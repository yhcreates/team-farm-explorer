        ❓ How to Play

        1. This farm is live and shared — everyone who opens this page joins the same farm and sees each other moving around in real time.
        2. Move with Arrow keys, WASD, or click anywhere to walk there automatically.
        3. Walk onto a soil plot (or click one) to till it, plant a crop, water it a few times, then harvest — or use the action buttons below, which light up automatically based on where you're standing.
        4. Harvested crops go into the team's shared Inventory. Feed the animals in the pen with them — chickens love corn 🌽, cows love carrots 🥕, sheep love strawberries 🍓.
        5. Keep animals happy and they'll reward the team with eggs 🥚, milk 🥛, and wool 🧶.
        6. Visit the Kudos Board near the farmhouse to leave a message of appreciation for a teammate.
        7. Open 🏗️ Build to spend the team's harvest points on farm decorations that unlock as everyone plays.
        8. Use 🎨 My Farmer to change your own name or look anytime.
